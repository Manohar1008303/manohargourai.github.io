CLO Analytics — SQL & BI

An end-to-end CLO analytics project built in SQL and BI on a synthetic book of four CLO deals — featuring Golub Capital as the flagship — spanning 700 syndicated loans and ~$2B of collateral. It models the full servicing workflow: indenture compliance testing (OC/IC coverage, WARF, and concentration limits), the interest payment waterfall from senior tranches down to equity, portfolio and credit analytics, and trustee-versus-internal reconciliation with aged-break escalation, plus a dedicated single-client “client 360” on the Golub deal. Results surface through analytical SQL, reporting views, and an interactive dashboard, with a Power BI build guide covering the star schema and DAX measures.
